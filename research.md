# GTA 6 Instagram competitor research

**Accounts:** @houseofgta6 · @gta6.only · @grandtheftvi
**Window:** roughly Oct 18 – Nov 8, 2026 (the last three weeks before the Nov 19 launch)
**Method:** looked at each profile in Chrome (view only, nothing liked, followed or commented). Likes and comments come from the grid hover counts, Reel views from each account's Reels tab, and post times from the post timestamps. Times are converted to US Eastern (ET = UTC−5).

---

## TL;DR: what to copy

1. **Make it a comparison or a "spot the difference".** The biggest posts across all three accounts are side-by-sides: GTA 6 vs real life, GTA 5 vs GTA 6, game size vs other games. People comment their guess and share it.
2. **Put the whole idea on the image in a few bold words.** For example "Guess which one is real life and which is GTA 6", "~110 GB / ~111 GB / ~210 GB", "20 DAYS LEFT". The caption explains; the image stops the scroll.
3. **Reels get the reach, image posts get the likes.** gta6.only Reels usually pull 100–250K views, with spikes of 400K–1.1M. houseofgta6's highest-liked posts are mostly single images and carousels.
4. **Run a daily series.** gta6.only's "N days to GTA VI" countdown Reels (made with @pixelmorrow) are some of its best posts for comments. Fans come back every day.
5. **End every caption with one question.** All three accounts do this ("Did you get it right?", "Physical or digital?", "Where will you be when GTA 6 launches?").
6. **Post during the morning and the evening.** Mornings are ~9–11am ET and evenings ~6–9pm ET. gta6.only drops its countdown at 8:30am ET.
7. **Keep hashtags minimal:** 1–3, always including #GTA6. None of these accounts uses hashtag walls.

### Hooks
- **"Follow @HouseofGTA6 for all things GTA 🤝"** as the *first line* on their viral image posts (3 of the top 5). They put the follow ask at the top on posts they expect to spread.

### Caption style
- **Long, written like a news article:** average about 1,200 characters, 3–5 short paragraphs, neutral wording ("reportedly", "has not officially confirmed").

- Tweet screenshots with the GTA VI logo on top of a gameplay still.
- A small House of GTA 6 logo watermark in the bottom-left corner.

### Hashtags
#GTA6 on every post. #GTAVI and #RockstarGames on about half. Sometimes #GTA. **Never more than 3–4.**

### Posting times (ET)
Two clusters: **9–11am ET** (the most posts, 14–16 UTC) and **6–9pm ET** (23–02 UTC). There are some afternoon posts too.

## 4. Cross-account patterns

### What gets the most views
- **Reels with "news you can see":** official footage details (turn signals, 1.7M), PS5 pre-load screen (1.1M), TV ads (408K). The clip *is* the proof.
- **Countdown Reels** keep a steady 150–340K views each and bring people back every day.

### What gets the most likes, shares and reposts
- **Side-by-side comparisons** (real vs game, GTA 5 vs 6, file sizes). These get the most reposts by far (1.2M on the GB graphic).
- **Celebrity or pop-culture crossovers** (Kodak Black, Travis Scott/Nike, South Park).
- **Relatable "launch day" fantasies** (3 hours cruising without doing a mission).

### What gets the most comments
- **Giveaways** (2,244 comments, but low likes, so the reach isn't organic).
- **"Guess" posts** (827 on "real vs GTA 6").
- **Personal questions about launch day**: physical vs digital, download time on *your* internet, "Who's coming with you?"
- **Uncertain or controversial claims** (no online mode yet, 167 comments on an otherwise average post).

1. **Daily countdown Reel at ~8:30am ET** until Nov 19: 7–12 s of trailer footage, a big "X DAYS LEFT" overlay, a one-line caption and a question. After launch, switch to "Day X in Leonida".
2. **2–3 comparison posts a week** (real Miami vs Vice City, GTA 5 vs 6, NPC/car detail), with one bold headline on the image: "Guess which one is GTA 6".
3. **Breaking-news carousels within an hour of a Rockstar drop**: cover slide with a red "NEWS" tag and a bold headline, plus "SWIPE FOR MORE".

## 7. Why we are not getting reach, and what small accounts do instead (Nov 10, 2026)

**Method and limits.** Instagram, YouTube and TikTok pages are blocked from the automation sandbox, and our API app has no insights or business-discovery permission. So this section rests on (a) our own 28 posts via the Graph API (likes and comments only, no views), (b) press coverage of Instagram's ranking rules found by web search, and (c) sections 1 to 6 above. No new competitor reels were read. Article bodies could not be opened, so rule details come from search summaries of TechCrunch, Social Media Today and others, not from Instagram's own page.

### 7.1 Our numbers

- 196 followers, 28 posts. Likes per post range from 1 to 22.
- The 20 posts before Nov 8 (owner-made): median 8 likes. The 8 posts since Nov 8 (templated: trailer cuts + headline): median 2.5 likes. The newer posts have had less time to collect likes, so the gap is overstated, but nothing templated has beaten the owner's casual posts.
- Best posts: an Oct 30 carousel (22 likes), "Po Rockstar, não vacila 😭" (18), "Caiu a ficha que falta apenas 1 mês" (17). All three are one-line, joke-or-feeling posts. None is a news headline.
- With no views data we cannot tell a hook problem from a distribution problem. Getting insights is step one (7.4).

### 7.2 The most likely cause: Instagram does not recommend reposted footage

Since April 30, 2026 Instagram removes accounts that mostly repost other people's content from recommendations (Reels tab, Explore, suggested posts). Followers still see the posts. With 196 followers, recommendations are the only route to growth, so this alone would explain flat reach.

As reported:
- Counts as original: something you made yourself, or third-party material "materially edited": turned into a meme, narrated with a new voiceover, remixed to express a reaction, a parody compilation.
- Does **not** count: a watermark, logo, border, subtitles, a credit in the caption, a speed change, a screenshot.
- Identical or near-identical copies get replaced in recommendations by the original post.
- Eligibility comes back when most posts in a rolling 30 days are original. The status is shown in the app under Account Status.

Our pm Reel recipe (Rockstar trailer cuts, per-cut speed changes, a headline, our avatar on top, "Imagens: Rockstar Games") is close to a list of the edits that do not count. Thousands of pages are cutting the same 90 seconds of trailer, and @rockstargames posted it first.

This also explains section 1 to 6 better than "comparisons win": every top post there has something of the poster's own in it. The turn-signal Reel points at a detail. The 110/111/210 GB graphic is their own design. The pre-load Reel is their own PS5 screen recording. Davy Jones is on camera. Countdown reels over plain trailer footage only work for pages that already have 270K followers to feed.

### 7.3 What breaks out from a small account

- **Sends.** Mosseri has named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top signals, with sends mattering most for reaching non-followers. A post gets sent when it says something the viewer wants a specific friend to see: "eu e você dia 19", "manda pro amigo que vai faltar no trabalho".
- **Being first with something visible.** Screen recordings of a real thing (PS Store page, pre-load bar, the install size) carried the biggest reels in section 2.
- **One relatable line in PT-BR.** Davy Jones's formula, and also our own top three posts.
- **AI fake "gameplay" is getting millions of views. Do not copy it.** Rockstar is issuing takedowns, the playbook forbids misleading claims, and those accounts are being reported.
- Small accounts get proportionally more reach than big ones (Social Insider 2026: 9.8% reach rate at 1K to 5K vs 7.1% at 10K to 50K). Size is not what is holding us back.

What this says:
1. **Reels are being recommended.** About 73% of the Reel's views came from the Reels tab and Explore, so roughly 140 views from strangers. Instagram tests each Reel on a batch of 100 to 150 people and then stops.
2. **It stops at the first gate: skip rate.** Instagram lists skip rate first "in order of importance to reach", and 47.8% of people swiped away at the start. One share in 135 viewers gave it no second push.
3. **The opening frame is the likely reason.** The Reel opens on a parked boat with nobody in it, under a black header that takes about 40% of the screen. That breaks our own rule to open on a face, and the header makes it look like a template before the footage registers.
4. **Images and Stories reach almost nobody.** The image reached 10 people out of 196 followers and no strangers; the Story got 7 views. The followers are cold. These formats cannot grow the page at this size.
5. **No follows from 135 viewers**, so the profile fixes in 7.6 still matter once reach improves.

Changes this adds to 7.4:

- First frame: full-screen footage, a face or something moving, the hook text over the picture. No black header band, no avatar block.
